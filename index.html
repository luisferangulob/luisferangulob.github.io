01 / ABOUT

Statistics gives me the language. Computation gives me the tools.

I am a senior at the University of Arizona studying Statistics & Data Science, with minors in Computer Science and Microbiology.

My work sits at the intersection of quantitative modeling, computation, and biology. I am interested in using statistical and computational methods to understand complex systems, particularly problems involving biomedical data, survival, disease, and biological structure.

I currently conduct computational research developing and analyzing stochastic three-dimensional models of plant root architecture, while independently exploring applications of statistics and machine learning to biomedical questions.
