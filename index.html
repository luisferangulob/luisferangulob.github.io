01 / ABOUT

Statistics gives me the language. Computation gives me the tools.

I am a senior at the University of Arizona studying Statistics & Data Science, with minors in Computer Science and Microbiology.

My work combines quantitative modeling, computation, and biology. I am interested in using statistical and computational methods to understand complex systems, with a focus on biomedical data, survival, disease, and biological structure.

02 / RESEARCH

Modeling biological structure through computation.

My current research focuses on computational models of three-dimensional root architecture and the large-scale analysis of simulated biological structures.

The project combines stochastic modeling, geometry, simulation, and high-performance computing to explore how local growth rules generate complex root systems.

Animated 3D root architecture simulation

Featured Research

3D Root Architecture Simulation

Computational Plant Science Laboratory · University of Arizona

I develop a stochastic framework for generating and analyzing three-dimensional root architectures using local coordinate systems, quaternion-based transformations, branching, self-avoidance, and large-scale Monte Carlo simulation.

The work produces geometric representations of root systems that can be analyzed across thousands of parameter configurations and compared through quantitative structural measurements.
